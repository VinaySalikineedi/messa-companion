1. Who we are

Messa is an AI personal assistant you reach by text (SMS/iMessage), email, and soon WhatsApp. This Privacy Policy applies to everyone who texts, emails, or otherwise interacts with Messa ("we," "us," "our"), currently operated as a sole proprietorship.

By texting, emailing, or otherwise chatting with Messa, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you don't agree, please don't use the service -- and you can always tell Messa to stop, or email us to have your data removed (see Section 7).

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us directly

  • Phone number -- required, since Messa reaches you by text.
  • Name -- asked once, early on, so Messa can address you and provision your own @__PRIMARY_DOMAIN__ email address.
  • Email address and city or general location -- asked conversationally, whenever they're actually useful (for example, a weather- or timezone-dependent request, or emailing something to you) -- never required to keep using Messa, and you can tell her to skip either.
  • Anything else you tell Messa -- requests, preferences, documents or files you send her, and any other detail you share in the course of a conversation.

2.2 Information created by using Messa

  • Conversation history -- the full content of every message you exchange with Messa over SMS, iMessage, or her own email address, kept so she has context in later conversations.
  • A memory profile -- a running summary Messa keeps about you (preferences, recurring context) so she doesn't make you repeat yourself. This is used only to personalize replies to you, isolated from every other user's data.
  • Web-browsing task records -- when Messa browses the web or a website on your behalf (booking something, checking a price, filling out a form), a transcript of that session is kept so the task can be resumed or reviewed.
  • Reminders, tasks, calendar entries, and contacts you ask her to create or manage on Messa's own built-in tools.

2.3 Location information

Messa does not track your device's GPS location. If you tell her a city, she resolves it to an approximate latitude/longitude so she can give accurate local weather, timezone-aware reminders, and location-based recommendations. This comes only from what you tell her, not from your device or network.

2.4 Third-party accounts you connect

If you connect another account -- Gmail, Google Calendar, Todoist, Slack, Notion, or any of the 1,400+ apps Messa can reach -- she accesses only what that account's permissions grant, only to do what you asked. Disconnecting an account (just tell Messa) revokes that access going forward.

2.5 Credentials you ask Messa to manage

If you ask Messa to generate or store a login/password for a specific website (not one of the OAuth-connected apps above), it is encrypted before it's ever written to our database -- see Section 6.

2.6 Website and technical log data

When you visit our website (textmessa.com) or view a public live-view link, our web servers automatically record standard technical log data (such as your IP address, browser user-agent, and request timestamp). We use this information strictly to maintain security, detect and prevent abuse, and enforce fair-use limits.

3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Actually do what you ask -- draft and send email, manage your calendar and reminders, browse the web, research something, connect and use other apps on your behalf.
  • Personalize Messa's replies to you over time, using your memory profile.
  • Deliver messages and emails reliably, and keep a record you and Messa can both refer back to.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the service, including enforcing fair-use limits so the service stays available and preventing abuse.
  • Communicate with you about your account or the service itself.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your conversations with Messa to serve you advertising.

We do not use your personal messages, conversation history, or private documents to train public foundation AI models, and we configure our AI model provider integrations to opt out of training where supported.

5. How long we keep information

We keep account information, conversation history, email content, and browsing-task records for as long as your account is active, so Messa can keep the context that makes her useful. We are actively building automated retention and self-service deletion controls; in the meantime, you can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing __PRIVACY_EMAIL__, and we will action that request manually.

Encrypted site credentials (Section 2.5) are kept until you ask Messa to remove them, or until you request account deletion.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including:

  • Any website password Messa generates or stores for you is encrypted at rest using authenticated symmetric encryption, with the decryption key held separately in our infrastructure's secrets -- never in the database itself. This means a database copy alone cannot recover a stored password.
  • Data in transit between you, Messa, and the service providers in Section 4 is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Your memory profile is access-isolated at the database level so it can only be read back for you, not any other user.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your information, we will notify you as required by applicable law.
