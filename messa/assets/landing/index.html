Trust and control

Agentic does not mean unsupervised

Giving an assistant real capability is only worth it if you are still the one in charge.

  • She proposes, you confirm

    Scheduling something, spending money, sharing personal information: Messa lays out exactly what she is about to do and waits for your yes.

  • Nothing happens off screen

    Every browsing session has a live link. Watch her click through a page in real time, the same way you would watch over someone's shoulder.

  • Stops at a login wall for you

    Hit a real login, CAPTCHA, or one time code? She pauses and lets you know instead of guessing your credentials.

  • Disconnect or switch any app, any time

    Change your mind about a connected account? Tell her. She disconnects and reconnects a fresh one over text, with no settings page to hunt for.

Questions

Good to know

Is this actually a real person or an AI?
Messa is an AI, but one built to actually do things, not just chat about them. She browses real websites, books real things, and manages a real calendar, all with your OK before anything that matters.
Will she ever spend money or book something without asking me first?
No. Anything that spends money, schedules something, or shares personal information gets proposed to you first. She waits for an explicit yes before it actually happens.
How many apps can she actually connect to?
1,400+ today: Gmail, Google Calendar, Outlook, Slack, Notion, GitHub, Todoist, Asana, ClickUp, Reddit, Instagram and far more. If you connect two apps that do the same job, she will ask which one should be primary, and you can change that any time.
What happens if she hits a login page or a CAPTCHA?
She stops and tells you rather than guessing a password or trying to push through it, the same way a careful human assistant would hand it back to you.
Do I need to install anything?
No app, no account creation up front. Save the number, send a text, and you are talking to her, over iMessage, SMS or RCS, whatever your phone already does.
Can I disconnect an app or switch accounts?
Yes, just tell her. She will disconnect it, or disconnect and reconnect a fresh link for a different account, all over text. No settings page to go find.
