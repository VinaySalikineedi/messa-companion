Trust & control

Agentic doesn't mean unsupervised

Giving an assistant real capability is only worth it if you're still the one in charge.

  • She proposes, you confirm

    Scheduling something, spending money, sharing personal information — Messa lays out exactly what she's about to do and waits for your yes.

  • Nothing happens off-screen

    Every browsing session has a live link. Watch her click through a page in real time, the same way you'd watch over someone's shoulder.

  • Stops at a login wall for you

    Hit a real login, CAPTCHA, or one-time code? She pauses and lets you know instead of guessing your credentials.

  • Cancel or pause anything, anytime

    A recurring routine, a briefing, an ongoing task — one text turns it off. Nothing runs that you can't easily stop.

Questions

Good to know

Is this actually a real person or an AI?
Messa is an AI — but one built to actually do things, not just chat about them. She browses real websites, books real things, and manages a real calendar, all with your OK before anything that matters.
Will she ever spend money or book something without asking me first?
No. Anything that spends money, schedules something, or shares personal information gets proposed to you first — she waits for an explicit yes before it actually happens.
What happens if she hits a login page or a CAPTCHA?
She stops and tells you rather than guessing a password or trying to push through it — the same way a careful human assistant would hand it back to you.
Do I need to install anything?
No app, no account creation up front. Save the number, send a text, and you're talking to her — over iMessage, SMS, or RCS, whatever your phone already does.
Can I stop a recurring automation or a briefing?
Yes — just tell her. Any routine, briefing, or automation she's running can be paused or cancelled with a text, any time.
What can't she do yet?
She's honest when something's outside what she can do rather than guessing — and we're actively expanding her team of specialists, so if there's something you wish she could handle, tell her. That's exactly how we decide what to build next.
